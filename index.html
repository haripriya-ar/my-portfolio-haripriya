About

A little about me

For as long as I can remember, I have been drawn to understanding how things work — not just the surface-level mechanics, but the deeper patterns and logic underneath. That curiosity eventually led me to computer science, and then specifically to artificial intelligence, where I found a field that felt both endlessly challenging and deeply rewarding.

What excites me most is the intersection of AI and real-world problems. During my time at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, I have worked on projects ranging from glaucoma detection using explainable deep learning to sensor-based tremor detection for Parkinson's patients. These experiences taught me that the most valuable technology is the kind that genuinely helps people.

I gravitate towards work that requires both rigorous technical thinking and empathy — understanding not just the algorithms, but the context in which they will be used. I enjoy reading research papers, prototyping ideas, and collaborating with people who share a commitment to building things that matter.

Outside of academics, I find energy in community work, organizing events, and learning from the people around me. I believe good research comes from staying curious, staying grounded, and being willing to ask questions that don't have easy answers.

Natural Language Processing
01

LLM-Enhanced Explainable Biomedical Relation Extraction: Pipeline vs Joint Models with RAG-Based Evidence Retrieval

Problem

Extracting meaningful drug–disease relationships from vast biomedical literature is time-consuming and error-prone when done manually. Existing NLP methods often lack interpretability, making their outputs difficult for researchers and clinicians to trust or verify.

Solution

Developed an advanced biomedical NLP system using transformer-based language models (BioBERT) for named entity recognition and relation extraction. Compared pipeline and joint learning architectures, and integrated Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) with ChromaDB for evidence retrieval. Added explainability layers using SHAP, LIME, and attention visualization, with LLM-powered clinical explanation generation.

Key Contributions

  • Biomedical NER using BioBERT
  • Drug–Disease Relation Extraction
  • Pipeline vs Joint Learning comparison
  • RAG with ChromaDB vector database
  • Explainable AI (SHAP & LIME)
  • Attention visualization
  • LLM-powered clinical explanations
  • Biomedical QA interface
  • Interactive Gradio deployment

Outcome

Designed an interpretable biomedical knowledge extraction framework that helps researchers identify meaningful drug–disease relationships from large volumes of scientific literature, deployed as an interactive Gradio application.

Python BioBERT PyTorch Transformers ChromaDB RAG LLMs SHAP LIME Gradio

Healthcare AI
03

Smart Glove for Parkinson's Tremor Detection and Assistance

Problem

Parkinson's patients experience tremors that significantly affect daily activities, but continuous monitoring in clinical settings is impractical. There is a need for wearable, non-intrusive solutions that can track symptoms in real time.

Solution

Developed a sensor-equipped smart glove that captures hand movement data. Applied deep learning models to analyze tremor patterns and classify severity, enabling real-time assistive vibration feedback for the wearer.

Key Contributions

  • Sensor-based tremor detection
  • Deep learning classification
  • Real-time monitoring pipeline
  • Assistive vibration feedback
  • Healthcare-focused wearable design

Outcome

Produced a functional prototype demonstrating wearable AI for continuous health monitoring. Featured and demonstrated at the AI Impact Summit conducted by Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham (January 2026).

Medical AI
04

Multimodal Explainable Deep Learning Framework for Glaucoma Detection and Risk Prediction

Problem

Early glaucoma detection remains challenging, especially in resource-limited settings where specialist access is scarce. Existing automated systems often lack transparency, making it difficult for clinicians to trust their predictions.

Solution

Built a multimodal framework combining retinal fundus image analysis with clinical data. Integrated Grad-CAM-based explainability to provide visual heatmaps highlighting the regions driving each prediction, making the model's reasoning transparent to healthcare professionals.

Key Contributions

  • CNN-based feature extraction
  • Clinical data fusion
  • Explainable AI using Grad-CAM
  • Medical image analysis pipeline
  • Healthcare decision support tool

Outcome

Created a working decision-support tool that provides both risk predictions and interpretable explanations, designed to assist ophthalmologists in clinical workflows.

Python Deep Learning Computer Vision Medical Imaging Grad-CAM
